Faith & Inspiration

This page shares reflections on faith, purpose, and the deeper lessons we can see in the game of chess— without pressure, without preaching—simply offering perspective for those who want it.

Chess & Christian Principles

  • The pawn’s journey as perseverance and transformation
  • Sacrifice leading to victory
  • The King as the center of all strategy
  • Light vs darkness on the board
  • Every piece has purpose—just like the Body of Christ

Historical Note

In 1474, a Dominican friar wrote The Book of the Morals of Chess, using the game to teach moral lessons. The text is now public domain and available in online archives.
