How it works

What a lesson looks like

Every part of the game: openings, active middlegame play and endgames.

We play and talk

In the first lesson we play, look at a few positions and talk about your goals, so I know exactly where you are.

We build your plan

Openings you understand, middlegame ideas you can use, and the endgames that turn good positions into wins.

About your coach

Mark Allen

I'm a USCF Expert-rated player, and I've been playing and coaching chess for decades. I play tournament chess in the US and internationally.

My first rule is simple: chess is a game, and above all it should be fun. From there we build every part of your game: openings, active middlegame play and endgames.

As you improve, the lessons become more and more your own, built around your games, your goals and the way you learn best.
